KISHORE KUMAR

Kishore Kumar(born Abhas Kumar Ganguly;4 August 1929-13 October 1987) was an Indian Playback singer,musician and actor.
He is widel regarded as one of the greatest,most influenctial and dynamic singer in history of modern Indian music.Kumar was one
of the most popular singers in the Indian subcontinent,notable for his yodelling and ability to sing songs in different voices.
He used to sing songs in different genres but some of his rare compositions,considering classics,werepedia.or lost in time.In 2013,Kumar
was voted "The most popular Male Playback singer" in a poll conducted by the Filmfare magzine.
